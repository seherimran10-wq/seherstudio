Your worst enemy is you, a few seconds ago. Every orb you grab leaves behind an echo that replays the route you just ran, back and forth, forever.

  • You. Steer with the mouse, drag a finger anywhere, or use WASD / arrows.
  • Grab orbs to fill the level bar. Each level makes echoes faster and orbs harder to catch.
  • Echoes loop your old routes. Touch one and the run ends. Skim close for graze points.
  • From level 3, hunters chase you down. They burn out after a while.
  • Every 5th orb is a prism. It shatters your oldest echo.
