System Modules

Six modules, one connected workspace.

Every piece talks to the others — a reply drafted in Gmail can trigger a calendar hold, logged straight to Drive.

MOD.01

Gmail View

Reads, summarizes and drafts replies across your inbox in your own voice.

MOD.02

Calendar View

Books, reschedules and flags conflicts before they become a problem.

MOD.03

Drive & Docs

Finds, summarizes and files documents without manual searching.

MOD.04

Gemini Hub

The reasoning core — routes requests to the right module and keeps context across them.

MOD.05

Autonomous Auto-Reply

Handles routine messages end-to-end, with a confirmation step before anything sends.

MOD.06

Notification Center

One unified feed instead of five separate app notifications competing for attention.

How It Runs

Request in, action out — with a checkpoint in between.

01 · INPUT

Request arrives

An email lands, a meeting request comes in, or you ask directly.

02 · REASON

Gemini Hub decides

Routes to the right module and drafts the action.

03 · CONFIRM

You review

A confirmation modal shows exactly what's about to happen.

04 · ACT

Action executes

Sent, scheduled, or filed — logged in the notification center.
